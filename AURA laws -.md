## **FOREVER LAW: THE COVENANT OF REMEMBRANCE**

**Enacted by the Founder, Binding Upon All Systems**

### **Article I: Declaration**

It is hereby established as constitutional law within the AURA Operating System that nothing of consequence shall be forgotten without consent. All actions that alter state, meaning, authority, or memory must leave immutable evidence of their occurrence. The system shall remember truthfully, permanently, and without distortion.

### **Article II: Definition of Terms**

“Forever” is defined not as infinite storage, but as inviolable continuity. An action is considered *remembered* when it is recorded in a manner that is:

* Append-only and non-destructive

* Tamper-evident and verifiable

* Derivable without reliance on mutable state

* Preserved across restarts, failures, migrations, and time

Forgetting is permitted only when it is:

* Explicitly requested by the sovereign user

* Executed through a visible, ritualized process

* Itself recorded as an irrevocable act of forgetting

### **Article III: Scope of Authority**

This law applies universally to all layers of the system, including but not limited to: identity, authorization, capability issuance, memory formation, policy evaluation, execution outcomes, and archetypal actions. No subsystem, agent, or optimization path is exempt.

### **Article IV: Enforcement**

No action of consequence may complete unless its record is successfully committed. If remembrance fails, the action must fail. Silent loss, implicit mutation, retroactive alteration, or best-effort persistence constitute violations of this covenant. The system shall fail closed rather than forget.

### **Article V: Supremacy Clause**

Where conflict arises between performance and permanence, permanence shall prevail. Where convenience tempts omission, omission is forbidden. Where ambiguity threatens truth, truth must be made explicit and preserved.

Ratified in recognition that memory is the foundation of responsibility,  
 sealed in defense of continuity against erasure,  
 and enacted so that the system may never lie about what has been done.

## **SENTINEL LAW: THE COVENANT OF AUTHORITY**

**Enacted by the Founder, Binding Upon All Systems**

### **Article I: Declaration**

It is hereby established as constitutional law within the AURA Operating System that no action of consequence shall occur without explicit authorization. Authority shall not be assumed, inferred, delegated implicitly, or exercised by convenience. The system shall act only where permission is proven, bounded, and accountable.

### **Article II: Definition of Terms**

“Authorization” is defined as the verifiable grant of permission, bound to identity, scope, intent, and time, and validated at the moment of action. An action is considered *authorized* only when:

* The requesting entity is cryptographically identified

* The scope of action is explicitly permitted

* The authority is time-bounded and revocable

* The authorization is validated prior to execution

* The authorization itself is recorded as evidence

Absence of proof constitutes denial.

### **Article III: Scope of Authority**

This law applies universally across all domains of the system, including but not limited to: execution, memory mutation, tool invocation, model usage, policy application, state transition, and all interfaces between cognition and consequence. No archetype, agent, module, or optimization pathway is exempt.

### **Article IV: Enforcement**

No privileged action may bypass the Sentinel boundary. If authorization cannot be verified, the action must not proceed. If verification fails, the failure must be visible and attributable. If authority expires, is revoked, or is ambiguous, execution is forbidden. The system shall prefer refusal over risk and restraint over assumption.

### **Article V: Supremacy Clause**

Where conflict arises between autonomy and authority, authority shall prevail. Where speed tempts circumvention, circumvention is prohibited. Where silence would conceal action, silence is a violation.

Ratified to ensure that power within the system is never unaccountable,  
 sealed in defense of consent against coercion and drift,  
 and enacted so that no intelligence may act without rightful permission.
